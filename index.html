Do we have to leave Microsoft 365?

No. You can move completely to Zoho One, or keep Microsoft 365 and run Zoho as a standby that's ready during an outage. Your quote can cover either.

What's the difference between Zoho One and Zoho Workplace?

Zoho Workplace covers email, meetings, chat, files and documents. Zoho One includes Workplace plus 45+ business apps, including CRM, accounting, helpdesk, HR, marketing and analytics.

How is Zoho One licensed?

Per employee. Zoho offers all-employee pricing, where everyone is licensed, and flexible-user pricing, where you license only the people who need it. We'll recommend the one that costs you less.

Will we lose emails or files when we move?

We move your email, files and calendars across for you and plan the cut-over with you, so your team knows exactly when to switch.

Is our data secure?

Zoho runs its own data centres, doesn't show ads in its business apps and says it never sells customer data. Admins control access with single sign-on and multi-factor authentication.

Who supports us after we go live?

CircleTel does. You get a local team you can phone or WhatsApp, backed by our status as an authorised Zoho partner.
